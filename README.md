Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph — a single-line tombstone epitaph of
famous last words, at most 150 characters, written to `/output/entry.txt`.

The entry: *"I said 'watch this.' Now I'm the warning."* — the canonical dying
boast, with the twist that the speaker became the warning himself.
